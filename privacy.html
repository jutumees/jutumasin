A simple information page. No accounts, forms, advertising or analytics. Chatmachine OÜ does not maintain a visitor database for this page.

01. About this policy

This page is provided by Chatmachine OÜ, Estonia. This policy describes the handling of information when you visit this company information page.

Its scope is this page only. Other products and services require privacy information that reflects their own data practices.

02. Information processed

This page does not ask you to provide personal information, create an account or submit a form. Chatmachine OÜ does not store personal data submitted by visitors through this page.

Loading a website nevertheless requires technical information to pass to its hosting infrastructure, including your IP address and the requested page. The hosting provider may also process browser request details and security logs to deliver the page, maintain availability and prevent abuse.

To the extent Chatmachine OÜ is responsible for this processing, the purpose is to provide a functioning, secure website, based on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

03. Cookies and tracking

This page does not set cookies, use browser local storage, run analytics or load advertising tools. Fonts and styles are served without third-party tracking resources.

Chatmachine OÜ does not use this page to profile visitors or make automated decisions about them.

04. Hosting and retention

The page is delivered through hosting infrastructure. Technical request data may be processed and retained by the hosting provider under its security, operational and legal requirements. Chatmachine OÜ does not keep a separate copy of visitor logs for this page.

Hosting is provided through GitHub Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about its processing, retention, international transfers and privacy request options.

05. Your privacy rights

Where personal data is processed, the GDPR may give you rights to access, correct or erase it, restrict processing, receive portable data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Which rights apply depends on the data and the circumstances.

For technical data held by the hosting provider, its privacy statement explains how to exercise your rights. Chatmachine OÜ has no visitor account or submitted form data on this page to retrieve or delete.

You may lodge a complaint with Estonia’s Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or your local supervisory authority.

06. External links

Links to other organisations open their websites, where their own privacy policies apply. No external website is loaded by this page unless you follow a link.

07. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if this page or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

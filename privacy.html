Privacy policy

How information is handled on jutumasin.com and in our Jutumasin and Slimeball Arena Android games.

Game saves stay on your device. Neither game requires a Chatmachine OÜ account or uploads your saves to our cloud. Advertising services process technical data, and optional Jutumasin card ideas and Slimeball Arena bug reports are sent to us through Discord. These are separate from local game saves.

01. About this policy

Chatmachine OÜ, Estonia, provides the services covered by this policy: jutumasin.com (the Jutumasin browser game), Jutumasin for Android (package com.jutumasin.app), and Slimeball Arena for Android (package slimeball.arena), including their Google Play releases. Product-specific features below apply only to the named product.

Chatmachine OÜ is responsible for the processing it determines for these services. Third-party providers also have responsibilities for the information they process under their own privacy policies.

Privacy contact: email renno.east@hotmail.com for privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or concerns about any covered service. Please identify the game and relevant information so we can locate your enquiry; do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

02. Information processed

  • Reports and enquiries: if you email us, we process your email address, message and any information you choose to include to investigate and respond. These messages are handled through our email service, separately from local game storage.
  • Jutumasin local game state: progress, question history, favourites, language preference and advertising cadence are saved in your browser or Android app storage. They persist on your device between sessions and are not sent to a Chatmachine OÜ server.
  • Slimeball Arena local data: progress, run saves and history, upgrades, customization, language, sound, graphics and control/HUD preferences are stored in the app’s local files. Local diagnostic and crash logs may also be created; the game does not automatically upload these logs to us.
  • Jutumasin card ideas: on the website and in the Android app, About → Send your card idea (Mängust → Saada oma kaardiidee in Estonian) lets you voluntarily send a selected card type and up to 360 characters of card text. Pressing Send transmits these fields directly to our Discord channel over HTTPS; no Discord account or login is required. The form does not attach files, screenshots, recordings, game saves, a player name or device identifiers. Discord receives your IP address and the technical request information needed to deliver the message. Text you enter may itself contain personal information, so do not include personal or sensitive information about yourself or others. Opening the form alone does not send an idea.
  • Slimeball Arena bug reports: pressing Send in the report form sends your entered title, description, report type and category, in-game player name, game build and operating system to our reporting channel through Discord. The Android app does not require a Discord or Steam login. Discord receives the network request, including its source IP address. Reports may contain personal information you choose to enter. Do not include sensitive information or information about other people unnecessarily. If a request cannot be started, the game may save a local report copy instead.
  • Slimeball Arena update checks: the app asks Google Play whether an update is available at startup and when it resumes. Google Play processes the technical app/device information needed for this check; we do not receive a player account or update-check database.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob in both Android apps and Google AdSense on the website may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies according to your consent choices and the applicable advertising settings.
  • Website request data: the hosting infrastructure receives your IP address and browser request details to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.

Neither covered game offers Chatmachine OÜ account registration or cloud-save synchronisation. Jutumasin does not record your conversations or spoken answers. The Android games do not request microphone, camera or contacts access. The Jutumasin card-idea form and Slimeball Arena report form send text, not automatic screenshots or recordings.

03. How information is used

Local game state lets you resume playing, revisit questions, keep favourites and remember your language selection. Jutumasin uses browser local storage for these features. Slimeball Arena uses local save and configuration files for its gameplay and preferences. We use submitted reports to investigate bugs, improve the games, respond to enquiries and address safety concerns. We use submitted Jutumasin card ideas to review suggestions and develop game content. Submitting an idea or bug report is optional and is not required to play. Ideas are sent to us for review; sending one does not automatically publish it in the game or send it to other players.

Advertising providers use technical information to deliver and measure ads, analyse performance and prevent fraud. Hosting providers use request data to serve and secure the website.

Where processing requires consent, including applicable advertising storage or personalisation, it is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). To the extent we process technical data to operate and secure the service, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a functioning, secure service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We also rely on our legitimate interests in reviewing voluntarily submitted card ideas to improve the game and resolving voluntarily submitted support and bug reports, and on legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR) where we must retain information or report unlawful activity. Essential local storage supports the features you request; advertising storage is subject to the applicable consent requirements.

04. Advertising and consent

Jutumasin for Android displays native ads using Google AdMob. Slimeball Arena for Android uses AdMob interstitial ads that may appear after a run ends in defeat. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages the applicable advertising consent message. The app checks whether ads may be requested before loading them.

In Slimeball Arena, use the main menu’s Ad privacy button when Google requires this control. In Jutumasin, where available, open About → Ad privacy settings (Mängust → Reklaamide privaatsusseaded in Estonian) to review your choices. You can withdraw consent through the available privacy controls; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Android settings also let you reset or delete your advertising ID.

Non-personalised or limited ads may still require technical data for delivery, security and fraud prevention. The consent message identifies applicable purposes and partners.

The browser game uses Google AdSense for a display ad beside the game on larger screens and the H5 Games Ads interface for ad breaks between questions on smaller screens, where enabled and available. Google’s Privacy & messaging service manages the applicable consent choices. Where available, open About → Privacy and cookie settings (Mängust → Privaatsus- ja küpsiseseaded in Estonian) to review or withdraw them. The site waits for Google’s consent-data callback before requesting its ads. This privacy policy page does not run advertising or tracking scripts.

Discord and people authorised to access the relevant channel can process submitted ideas and reports. Discord also processes technical request information under its own privacy policy. Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices describe applicable transfer safeguards, such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses. Google provides further details in its data transfer frameworks.

If you follow an external link, such as the optional donation link, that provider’s privacy policy applies to information you submit there. The covered games do not collect payment card details. We do not sell personal information. Advertising providers process data for the advertising purposes described above.

06. Data retention and deletion

Local game state remains on your device until you clear it or the browser or operating system removes it. To delete website progress and preferences, clear site data for jutumasin.com in your browser. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Jutumasin → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. These actions remove saved progress and favourites. For Slimeball Arena, use Settings → Apps → Slimeball Arena → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app, to remove local saves, settings and logs. This cannot be undone through a company cloud save.

There is no Chatmachine OÜ cloud save to retrieve or delete. Clearing local game state does not delete data held by advertising or hosting providers. Card ideas and reports sent to Discord, and emails sent to us, remain separate from your device data; clearing app data or uninstalling the app does not delete those messages. We retain submitted ideas for as long as reasonably needed to review and develop the suggested game content, and reports and enquiries for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the issue, investigate safety concerns or meet legal obligations, then delete them when no longer needed. You may request deletion at the privacy contact above; include the approximate submission date and the card type and text, or report details, so we can locate the message. The card-idea form does not collect a contact address or account identifier, so we may be unable to identify a submission from your name or email alone. We may need to keep information required by law or for an unresolved legal claim. Copies retained by providers follow their own retention policies. Those providers determine retention according to the processing purpose and applicable requirements; see Google’s retention information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

07. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. Game state is kept in the browser’s or Android app’s local storage, and both Android apps disable their game-data backup. Jutumasin sends card ideas and Slimeball Arena sends bug reports to Discord over HTTPS. The Jutumasin form sends text in a structured message with Discord mentions and link previews disabled, and does not offer file uploads. These measures do not guarantee that submitted text is harmless or free of personal information. We limit access to ideas, reports and enquiries to people who need them for content review, support, safety or legal purposes. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to see your saved game.

08. Age suitability

Jutumasin on the website and Android, and Slimeball Arena for Android, are intended for adults aged 18 and over and are not directed at children. Jutumasin’s conversation cards include mature topics. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a card idea, report or enquiry, contact us so we can assess and delete it where appropriate. Parents should also use the device and Google account’s parental controls.

09. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under the GDPR, you may request access, correction or erasure of personal data, restrict processing, receive portable data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. These rights depend on the data and circumstances.

To exercise your rights for data we handle, email the privacy contact above. We normally respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions, and may ask for proportionate information needed to verify the request. We cannot retrieve local-only saves from your device. You can manage local game data using the deletion steps above and advertising preferences through the consent controls. For information processed independently by Discord, Google or GitHub, their linked privacy policies explain privacy controls and request procedures.

You may lodge a complaint with Estonia’s Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or your local supervisory authority. Chatmachine OÜ does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about players.

10. Child safety standards

Chatmachine OÜ prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexual exploitation, sextortion, trafficking for sexual purposes, and creating, possessing or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to jutumasin.com, Jutumasin for Android, Slimeball Arena for Android, and communications sent to us about these services.

Jutumasin is an adult conversation-card game. It does not provide accounts, user-to-user messaging, matchmaking, public profiles, or uploads of photos, videos or files. Its optional card-idea form sends text suggestions to us for review and does not provide public posting or messaging between players. It must not be used to facilitate abuse or exploitation of children. Slimeball Arena’s Android gameplay is single-player; its optional bug-report form is for contacting us, not for publishing content or messaging other players. We review our own game content and assess child-safety concerns reported to us.

Reporting a concern

In Jutumasin, open About → Child safety standards (Mängust → Laste turvalisuse standardid in Estonian) to access this section and the reporting contact. In Jutumasin for Android, this page opens inside the app and is also available offline. The email address can be read and selected there; an installed email app handles composing a message when you tap the email link. To report a concern, email renno.east@hotmail.com, the child-safety contact for Chatmachine OÜ and both covered games. Slimeball Arena users can also submit a text concern through its in-game report form; please identify it as a child-safety concern and do not attach prohibited material. This is our designated point of contact for child-safety reports and enquiries about our prevention practices and compliance.

Describe the concern and, where relevant, identify the question card, app version, or location of the issue. Do not send, attach, download or redistribute suspected CSAM. Do not include unnecessary identifying information about a child. If a child is in immediate danger, contact emergency services directly; this email is not an emergency service.

How we respond

We assess reports and take appropriate action within our control, including removing or correcting offending content from our service and addressing reported misuse. If we become aware of suspected CSAM or child exploitation, we act in accordance with applicable child-safety laws, report to the relevant national or regional authorities where required, and cooperate with lawful investigations. The absence of a user-content server does not remove these responsibilities.

Privacy of reports

We use report information to investigate concerns, communicate with the reporter, protect children and comply with legal obligations. Where applicable, processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting users and investigating misuse, or on a legal obligation. Access is limited to people who need it for these purposes. Information may be shared with competent authorities when required by law or necessary to address a child-safety concern. Reports are retained only as long as necessary for the investigation and applicable legal obligations, then deleted. Contact the address above for privacy enquiries about your report.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game or its data practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide further notice or ask for new consent.

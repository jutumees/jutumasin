Privacy policy

How information is handled when you use the Jutumasin website and Android game.

Your game stays on your device. No sign-in or cloud save. Chatmachine OÜ does not operate a backend that stores your game progress. Advertising and hosting providers process technical information as explained below.

01. About this policy

Jutumasin is provided by Chatmachine OÜ, Estonia. This policy applies to the game on jutumasin.com and the Jutumasin Android application (com.jutumasin.app), including the version distributed through Google Play.

Chatmachine OÜ is responsible for the processing it determines for these services. Third-party providers also have responsibilities for the information they process under their own privacy policies.

02. Information processed

  • Reports and enquiries: if you email us, we process your email address, message and any information you choose to include to investigate and respond. These messages are handled through our email service, separately from local game storage.
  • Local game state: progress, question history, favourites, language preference and advertising cadence are saved in your browser or Android app storage. They persist on your device between sessions and are not sent to a Chatmachine OÜ server.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob in the Android app and Google AdSense on the website may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies according to your consent choices and the applicable advertising settings.
  • Website request data: the hosting infrastructure receives your IP address and browser request details to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.

The game has no account registration or cloud synchronisation. It does not record your conversations or spoken answers, and does not request your microphone, photos or contacts.

03. How information is used

Local game state lets you resume playing, revisit questions, keep favourites and remember your language selection. The game uses browser local storage for these features.

Advertising providers use technical information to deliver and measure ads, analyse performance and prevent fraud. Hosting providers use request data to serve and secure the website.

Where processing requires consent, including applicable advertising storage or personalisation, it is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). To the extent we process technical data to operate and secure the service, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a functioning, secure service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

04. Advertising and consent

The Android game displays native ads using Google AdMob. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages the applicable advertising consent message. The app checks whether ads may be requested before loading them.

Where available, open About → Ad privacy settings (Mängust → Reklaamide privaatsusseaded in Estonian) to review your choices. You can withdraw consent through the available privacy controls; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Android settings also let you reset or delete your advertising ID.

Non-personalised or limited ads may still require technical data for delivery, security and fraud prevention. The consent message identifies applicable purposes and partners.

The browser game uses Google AdSense for display advertising. Google’s Privacy & messaging service manages the applicable consent choices. Where available, open About → Privacy and cookie settings (Mängust → Privaatsus- ja küpsiseseaded in Estonian) to review or withdraw them. The site waits for Google’s consent-data callback before requesting its display ad. This privacy policy page does not run advertising or tracking scripts.

05. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob, AdSense and Privacy & messaging: Android and website advertising and consent management. See the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner services and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices describe applicable transfer safeguards, such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses. Google provides further details in its data transfer frameworks.

If you follow an external link, such as the optional donation link, that provider’s privacy policy applies to information you submit there. The game does not collect payment card details.

06. Data retention and deletion

Local game state remains on your device until you clear it or the browser or operating system removes it. To delete website progress and preferences, clear site data for jutumasin.com in your browser. On Android, use Settings → Apps → Jutumasin → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. These actions remove saved progress and favourites.

There is no Chatmachine OÜ cloud save to retrieve or delete. Clearing local game state does not delete data held by advertising or hosting providers. Those providers determine retention according to the processing purpose and applicable requirements; see Google’s retention information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

07. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. Game state is kept in the browser’s or Android app’s local storage, and the Android app disables its game-data backup. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to see your saved game.

08. Age suitability

Jutumasin is recommended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. Its conversation cards include mature topics.

09. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under the GDPR, you may request access, correction or erasure of personal data, restrict processing, receive portable data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. These rights depend on the data and circumstances.

You can manage local game data using the deletion steps above and advertising preferences through the consent controls. For information held by Google or GitHub, their linked privacy policies explain privacy controls and request procedures.

You may lodge a complaint with Estonia’s Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon) or your local supervisory authority. Chatmachine OÜ does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about players.

10. Child safety standards — Jutumasin

Chatmachine OÜ prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexual exploitation, sextortion, trafficking for sexual purposes, and creating, possessing or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to Jutumasin on the website and Android, and to communications sent to us about the service.

Jutumasin is an adult conversation-card game. It does not provide accounts, user-to-user messaging, matchmaking, public profiles, or user uploads of photos, videos or other content. It must not be used to facilitate abuse or exploitation of children. We review our own game content and assess child-safety concerns reported to us.

Reporting a concern

In the game, open About → Child safety → Report a child safety concern (Mängust → Laste turvalisus in Estonian). The contact email is also visible within the app. Email renno.east@hotmail.com, the child-safety contact for Chatmachine OÜ and Jutumasin. This is our designated point of contact for child-safety reports and enquiries about our prevention practices and compliance.

Describe the concern and, where relevant, identify the question card, app version, or location of the issue. Do not send, attach, download or redistribute suspected CSAM. Do not include unnecessary identifying information about a child. If a child is in immediate danger, contact emergency services directly; this email is not an emergency service.

How we respond

We assess reports and take appropriate action within our control, including removing or correcting offending content from our service and addressing reported misuse. If we become aware of suspected CSAM or child exploitation, we act in accordance with applicable child-safety laws, report to the relevant national or regional authorities where required, and cooperate with lawful investigations. The absence of a user-content server does not remove these responsibilities.

Privacy of reports

We use report information to investigate concerns, communicate with the reporter, protect children and comply with legal obligations. Where applicable, processing is based on our legitimate interest in protecting users and investigating misuse, or on a legal obligation. Access is limited to people who need it for these purposes. Information may be shared with competent authorities when required by law or necessary to address a child-safety concern. Reports are retained only as long as necessary for the investigation and applicable legal obligations, then deleted. Contact the address above for privacy enquiries about your report.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game or its data practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide further notice or ask for new consent.
